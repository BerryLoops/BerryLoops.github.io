How to order

  1. Fill your cart

    Add what you love, then open your cart. Choose the colour for each piece and enter your name, phone number, province and delivery address.

  2. Send your order on WhatsApp

    Tap the WhatsApp button. Your items and total are already filled in. We reply with our Easypaisa account details.

  3. Pay and send the screenshot

    Send the total through Easypaisa and share the screenshot in the same chat. We confirm and start packing.

About BerryLoops

Every piece is made by hand with soft yarn and a lot of patience. Because each one is crocheted individually, tiny differences in shape and colour are part of the charm. Have a colour or design in mind? Message us on WhatsApp and we will see what we can stitch for you.
